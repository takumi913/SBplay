Your iPhone.
At home in your car.

Maps, music and conversations on your car display. SBplay brings wired and wireless CarPlay to compatible Android head units, with the familiar DiAuto interface.

Download APK ↓How to install

No jailbreak, dongle, account or authentication server required.

For Lynk & Co CS11 head units running Android 4.4.3 (API 19, 32-bit ARM) only. Install SBplay on the head unit, not on the iPhone. Support depends on the car firmware and iOS version.

SBplay targets the Lynk & Co CS11 platform only; BYD/DiLink and other Android head units are not supported by this fork.

A familiar drive

01

Install on your car

  1. While parked, enable the CS11 head unit's debugging/installation method and install the SBplay APK.
  2. Enable Bluetooth and Wi-Fi on the iPhone and pair it with the car.
  3. For wireless CarPlay, enable the car's hotspot, select wireless mode in SBplay, then follow the phone prompts.
  4. For USB CarPlay, connect a data-capable cable, select USB mode and approve the iPhone prompts.

ADB is not required for everyday use. APK installation options depend on the car. No manual MAC address entry.
